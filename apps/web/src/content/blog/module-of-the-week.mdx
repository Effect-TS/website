Ever spent two weeks building a feature, only to discover Effect already had it?

You could've used that module...

You could've borrowed a few ideas...

You COULD have gone outside...

...now you just want your weekend back.

We can't do much about your weekend, but we are starting a new blog series called "Module of the Week".

Each week, we'll pick a lesser-known module or concept in Effect, show you how it works, and explain when you might reach for it. Consider this an intervention for anyone whose idea of a relaxing Saturday is implementing a semaphore.

We want you to have hobbies. Let us help you touch some more grass.
