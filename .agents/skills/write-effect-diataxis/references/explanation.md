# Explanation

Write understanding-oriented material that helps the reader connect concepts,
causes, constraints, and design choices.

## Define the question

- Use a real or implied why question to bound the discussion.
- Name the subject clearly. Prefer a title that still makes sense after the
  word "About".
- Establish what the reader should understand by the end, rather than what they
  should build or configure.

## Develop understanding

- Provide context and explain relationships between Effect concepts and the
  problems they address.
- Discuss causes, consequences, design decisions, constraints, alternatives,
  and tradeoffs when they improve understanding.
- Make connections to familiar TypeScript behavior and to adjacent Effect
  concepts.
- Use examples, diagrams, and comparisons as evidence. Do not turn them into a
  sequence of required actions.
- Allow multiple perspectives when the subject benefits from them, while
  keeping the discussion inside its stated scope.

## Keep other modes separate

- Do not use step-by-step instructions as the page's spine.
- Link to how-to guides for operational tasks and tutorials for managed
  practice.
- Leave exhaustive API facts to reference material instead of reproducing
  them.

## Review the explanation

Verify that the page answers its governing question, makes useful connections,
and leaves the reader with a clearer mental model rather than merely a list of
facts or actions.
