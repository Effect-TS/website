otel-desktop-viewer is a trace viewer that runs on your machine. From lesson 16
on, your programs send it spans and logs over OTLP on port 4318, and it shows
them at [http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000).
`pnpm run doctor --viewer` starts it the first time, and `pnpm viewer` starts
it after that. It keeps everything it receives until you stop it.

This page is a reference. Read it once now, then come back whenever a lesson
asks you to look at a span.

## Reading a trace

A **trace** is one piece of work from start to finish: one CLI command, one
HTTP request, or one interval tick. It's made of spans arranged in a tree.
Each span is a named, timed step, and every `Effect.fn("name")` you write
produces a span with that name.

The screenshot below is the lesson 16 checkpoint. It shows `events.submit`
from the CLI, with the expense rule stored, submitting `laptop.json`.

![The events.submit trace in otel-desktop-viewer: CLI spans in green, server spans in purple, Engine.execute selected with status Ok](../_assets/viewer-trace.png "One trace from reflex-cli into reflex-server")

Here's how to read it:

- The Traces tab lists traces by their root span, newest first. Pick one to
  open it. The sidebar toggle at the top left hides the list and gives the
  waterfall more room.
- The waterfall has one row per span. Indentation shows nesting.
  `Engine.execute` ran inside `Engine.handleEvent`, which ran inside the
  server's `POST`. The number in brackets is how many children a span has.
- The bar shows when the span started and how long it took, against the
  time axis at the top. The bar's colour is the service, not the result. Green
  is `reflex-cli` and purple is `reflex-server`.
- The details panel opens on the right when you select a row. It shows the
  span's duration, its `status.code`, and attributes such as `rule.id`. Logs
  written inside a span are listed under Events, and in the Logs tab.

## Failed spans

A span ends with the result of the effect it wraps. When that effect fails, or
dies with a defect, the span's status is Error.

![A rules.run trace with Engine.execute in red, status.code 2 (Error) and an exception event BadResource: FileSystem.writeFile (fixtures)](../_assets/viewer-error-span.png "An Engine.execute span that failed")

This trace comes from `rules run` against a rule whose `appendFile` path is
`fixtures`. That's a directory, so the write fails. Notice:

- The failed span, and the span it failed inside, are red. The trace header
  counts the errors (`1/2 err`), and the arrows next to the count step through
  them.
- `status.code` is `2 (Error)`. `status.message` and the `exception` event
  carry the error's message.
- The parent `POST` is not red. The engine turned the failure into a `failed`
  outcome, so the request itself succeeded. The run row says the same thing in
  its own words: `failed (io)`.

## Interrupted spans

An interrupted effect didn't fail, and it didn't finish either. It was stopped.
Its span ends with status Unset, which the viewer doesn't colour at all.

![A rules.run trace that took 1.24 s, Engine.execute selected with status.code 0 (Unset) and effect.fiber.interrupted true](../_assets/viewer-unset-span.png "An Engine.execute span that was interrupted")

This one comes from pressing Ctrl-C on the CLI while `rules run` was waiting
on an append that couldn't finish. The server noticed the client had gone and
interrupted the handler, and with it `Engine.execute` and `appendLine`.

- `status.code` is `0 (Unset)`. That's the same code an ordinary span has
  before anything sets it, so don't read Unset as success.
- `effect.fiber.interrupted` is `true`. This attribute is how you tell an
  interruption apart from everything else.
- The bar ends at the moment of interruption, so the duration tells you how
  long the work ran before it was stopped.

Until lesson 19, nothing writes a final row for an interrupted run, so its row
stays `started`. Lesson 19 adds the `interrupted` outcome, and removing a rule
in the middle of a poll produces exactly this picture.

| Lesson      | Where                  | What to look for                                                                                                                    |
| ----------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Setup to 15 | nothing yet            | The trace list is empty. Your program creates spans, but nothing sends them to the viewer until lesson 16.                          |
